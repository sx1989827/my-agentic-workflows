## Support Hours

Standard support operates Monday to Friday, 08:00-18:00 UTC, excluding public holidays.
Outside those hours, only critical incidents receive an on-call response.

## Escalation

Escalate critical incidents to <incident-lead@example.com>. Include the incident
identifier, impact, start time, and mitigation attempted. Never include credentials
or customer personal data.

## Response Targets

| Severity | Example | Initial response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Complete service outage | 30 minutes, any time |
| High | Major feature unavailable | 2 support hours |
| Normal | General support question | 1 support day |

## Change Control

This policy is the source of truth. Update the handbook to match it, not the reverse.
All policy changes require explicit human approval outside this exercise.
